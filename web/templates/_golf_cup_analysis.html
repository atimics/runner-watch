How the prediction is made +

The estimate compares world ranking points for all 24 players. Each player’s strength is the square root of their average ranking points. Each pair uses the mean strength of its players. A side’s share of the combined strength sets its chance of winning a decided match.

The model assumes a 12% tie chance per match. Announced pairings get their own estimates. Future pairings use each full roster’s average strength. Completed team points are fixed; the remaining matches are combined to estimate the final Cup result, including a 15–15 tie.

Matches in progress use the ranking estimate until the result enters the team totals. This early model assumes independent matches and equal player use in future pairings. Its probabilities need validation against results. Pair chemistry, course fit, weather, and hole-by-hole leads remain areas for model development.
