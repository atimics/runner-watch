RATi Trust

The RATi Rules

A stock or memecoin is Ratified when it meets every standard below that applies to it, on the facts RATi could read at the time. It is not an endorsement, a guarantee, advice or an audit opinion. RATi is a shadow auditor: these rules are inspired by audit quality practice, including PCAOB QC 1000, not issued under or approved by any audit regulator.

A version's digest is published while it is in force; its full text is published when it is replaced, and never edited. To check one: take the SHA-256 of each covered file, write one line per file, path hash, in path order, and take the SHA-256 of those lines. This page does that for every published version.
