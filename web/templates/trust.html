The RATi Rules

A stock or memecoin is Ratified when it meets RATi's basic standards on the facts RATi could read at the time. It is not an endorsement, a guarantee, advice or an audit opinion. RATi is a shadow auditor: these rules are inspired by audit quality practice, including PCAOB QC 1000, not issued under or approved by any audit regulator.

Checking a digest

For each covered file (the rulebook, the methodology and the rule code), take the SHA-256 of its contents. Write one line per file, path hash, in path order. The SHA-256 of those lines is the digest.
