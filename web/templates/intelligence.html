How to read it

A larger score means the event deserves faster review, not that the stock will rise. Form 4 code P means an open-market or private purchase. Grants, option exercises, and tax withholding are kept separate. Offering filings are marked as risk.

SEC data is the source of record. Yahoo price and volume are delayed confirmation data. Always open the filing and check spreads, halts, dilution history, and news before acting.

The learning loop saves the price seen when a filing arrives, then samples it after 1 hour, 1 day, and 5 days. These are delayed observations for future model evaluation, not proof that a filing caused the move.
