Who controls your data

RATi, available at runners.rati.chat, sports.rati.chat, and the legacy stonks.rati.foundation address, controls the account data described here. Contact privacy@cenetex.com for privacy requests or questions.

Data we keep

  • •Account and securityYour chosen account name, passkey public-key records, essential session records, and short-lived security challenges. We never receive your fingerprint, face scan, device PIN, or private passkey key.
  • •Things you save or publishAI-generated ticker comments, comments you write on sports games, public stock Calls, public sports paper picks, private trade-journal entries, cases, research requests, and reports you choose to create. A stock report freezes Flash's forecast, model version, starting price, and later scored result. Sports picks freeze the event, market odds, source time, and result.
  • •Persistent public avatarsEach account receives one random avatar name, visual seed, and research ability. The same avatar intentionally links comments across ticker and sports threads, so readers can link those comments together. This is pseudonymity, not anonymity from RATi: the server keeps the private account link for deletion and abuse controls, while public pages do not expose the account name. Stock Calls and sports paper picks still use one automatic random animal name.
  • •BillingStripe customer and subscription references, plan status, and signed webhook event IDs. Stripe handles card details; RATi does not store them.
  • •Local model routingYour routing policy, local model ID, connector name, one-way connector token hash, job status, and the report request and response needed to complete work you asked for. RATi never stores the optional key used by your local model server.
  • •Service securityShort-lived rate-limit keys and infrastructure logs may contain an IP address and request details. They are used only to keep the service working and safe.

How storage choices work

The cloud copy lets RATi show and update your saved work after passkey sign-in. Hosting encrypts it in transit and at rest, but RATi can read it to run the features you ask for.

A local vault is encrypted inside your browser with AES-GCM and a key made from your passphrase. The passphrase and key are never sent to RATi. Moving data saves the vault, opens it once to check it, and only then removes saved and published items from the live server. Account security, public identity, Flash balance, and billing records remain until you delete the account.

Local storage belongs to this browser profile. Clearing browser data or losing the device can erase it, so download the encrypted vault file if you need a second copy. Deleting the local vault cannot be undone.

Why we use it

We use account and saved-content data to provide the service you request. We use limited security data for our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and outages. We keep billing records where needed to perform a purchase and meet legal duties. We do not build behavioural profiles or use personal data for advertising.

Service providers and transfers

Fly.io hosts the app and production data in San Jose, United States. Stripe processes payments. OpenRouter and the selected model provider process the evidence included in a research request or AI-drafted comment. Comment requests include the avatar's research ability, but not its public name, account name, or a RATi user fingerprint. OpenRouter requests require zero-data-retention routing.

If you choose a local model, your authenticated connector downloads the frozen evidence for your queued report and sends it to the model server you control. RATi does not contact that local server. Reports made by a customer model stay private and do not enter Flash's public performance record.

These providers may process data outside the EEA, UK, or Switzerland. RATi must keep processor terms and an approved transfer safeguard, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, on file before serving people in those regions.

How long we keep data

  • •No passive historyVisitor, view, dwell, seen, notification, and reaction records are not collected. The old tracking tables have been permanently removed.
  • •Security recordsPasskey challenges expire after 5 minutes, unfinished accounts after 15 minutes, and login sessions after 30 days.
  • •Your contentAccount content stays until you delete it or the account. A deleted account leaves its old public animal name as an unusable tombstone.
  • •Payments and backupsWebhook event IDs are kept for up to 400 days. Providers may retain transaction records when law requires it. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before expiry.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, erase, restrict, or receive your data, or object to processing. You may complain to your local data-protection authority. We answer verified requests without undue delay and normally within one month.
