PRIVACY BY LESS DATA

We do not track what you read.

RATi does not use analytics, advertising pixels, visitor IDs, reaction profiles, view logs, dwell logs, or cross-thread public identities.

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

Who controls your data

RATi, available at runners.rati.chat, sports.rati.chat, and the legacy stonks.rati.foundation address, controls the account data described here. Contact privacy@rati.foundation for privacy requests or questions.

Data we keep

  • •Account and securityYour chosen account name, passkey public-key records, essential session records, and short-lived security challenges. We never receive your fingerprint, face scan, device PIN, or private passkey key.
  • •Things you save or publishAI-generated ticker comments, public stock Calls, public sports paper picks, private trade-journal entries, cases, research requests, and reports you choose to create. Sports picks freeze the event, market odds, source time, and result.
  • •Private public namesComments use a stable random emoji name in each ticker thread. Stock Calls and sports paper picks use one automatic random animal name. Public pages do not expose the account behind either name.
  • •BillingStripe customer and subscription references, plan status, and signed webhook event IDs. Stripe handles card details; RATi does not store them.
  • •Service securityShort-lived rate-limit keys and infrastructure logs may contain an IP address and request details. They are used only to keep the service working and safe.

Why we use it

We use account and saved-content data to provide the service you request. We use limited security data for our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and outages. We keep billing records where needed to perform a purchase and meet legal duties. We do not build behavioural profiles or use personal data for advertising.

Service providers and transfers

Fly.io hosts the app and production data in San Jose, United States. Stripe processes payments. OpenRouter and the selected model provider process the evidence included in a research request. OpenRouter requests require zero-data-retention routing and do not contain a RATi user fingerprint.

These providers may process data outside the EEA, UK, or Switzerland. RATi must keep processor terms and an approved transfer safeguard, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, on file before serving people in those regions.

How long we keep data

  • •No passive historyVisitor, view, dwell, seen, notification, and reaction records are not collected. The old tracking tables have been permanently removed.
  • •Security recordsPasskey challenges expire after 5 minutes, unfinished accounts after 15 minutes, and login sessions after 30 days.
  • •Your contentAccount content stays until you delete it or the account. A deleted account leaves its old public animal name as an unusable tombstone.
  • •Payments and backupsWebhook event IDs are kept for up to 400 days. Providers may retain transaction records when law requires it. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before expiry.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, erase, restrict, or receive your data, or object to processing. You may complain to your local data-protection authority. We answer verified requests without undue delay and normally within one month.

Cookies

RATi sets one essential login cookie after you sign in. It lasts up to 30 days, is HTTP-only, SameSite=Lax, and secure in production. There are no analytics or advertising cookies, so there is no tracking-consent banner.
