# Persistent comment avatars

The comment avatar is a durable public actor, not decoration.

## Identity contract

- One account receives one random public name and one visual seed.
- The seed anchors the avatar's appearance so it does not drift between pages or later art versions.
- One research ability stays with the avatar and changes the emphasis of its Flash-written comments.
- The account name stays private, but the avatar deliberately makes public comments linkable across ticker threads.
- Level starts at 1. Later levels must be earned from recorded actions, not payment or model-written claims.

The first abilities are Catalyst Scout, Risk Sentinel, Filing Sleuth, Pattern Mapper, Liquidity Reader, and Countervoice. They are narrow evidence lenses. They do not create facts, improve access to private data, or provide financial advice.

## Design sources

Cosyworld treats an avatar as one actor whose identity, appearance, ability, and progression are anchored in shared state. AI can add voice, but it cannot rewrite the actor's name, history, or earned powers. Kyro keeps one identity and voice across surfaces rather than becoming a different character in each channel. Ratibot makes ability credible through visible action and an auditable record.

RATi follows the same pattern in a smaller form: the database owns the name, seed, ability, and level; the model only uses the assigned ability as a writing lens.

## Future game: Signal Hunt

The larger world can build on this identity layer:

1. A hidden treasure is revealed through source-backed market, company, or world clues.
2. Humans form opt-in teams and complete research challenges or puzzles.
3. Avatar abilities give each teammate a useful role, so mixed teams are stronger.
4. Rivalry and betrayal are explicit game actions with visible rules and a permanent audit trail. They must never expose private identity, enable market manipulation, or disguise real financial coordination.
5. Public deeds, promises, debts, discoveries, and completed challenges can unlock later levels and appearance changes.

The system should reward good evidence and cooperation, not time-on-platform. Every autonomous action needs a rate limit, cooldown, and visible record.
