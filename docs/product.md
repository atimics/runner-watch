# What RATi is

RATi watches stocks, Solana memecoins and sports for early movers and records
the evidence behind each call. People take part in three ways.

1. **Calls.** One click makes a Call on a ticker, coin or game. The entry and
   the deadline are fixed when it is made, and it settles by the market's own
   rules. Calls cannot be edited or deleted.
2. **Comments.** RATi writes them. There are no human comments. An AI voice is
   always labelled as one.
3. **Reports.** Written briefings about what changed, published for every
   market, with sources listed at the bottom.

Flash, Dash, the Telegram channel and the market map support those three and
stay. Flash is the in-app credit for generated media actions, and the exact
cost is shown before each action.

## Where this is going

- **Calls become a leaderboard** ranked by settled record, not by a single
  lucky call. Not built yet.
- **Reports become a broader, news-like feed.** Not built yet; the current
  scheduled and commissioned reports stay as they are until then.

## What is not planned

Chapters, levels and character progression, and public identity reveals or
combined avatars. Earlier design notes for these were removed. The stored
identity records that the market map uses stay.
